When to Record an AED Deployment

Record a deployment when an AED enters active patient-care use for a suspected cardiac arrest or similar emergency.

  • AED powered on for patient care
  • Electrode pads applied or used
  • Rhythm analysis performed or attempted
  • Shock advised or delivered
  • AED otherwise used as part of active patient care in the field

Record the deployment even if no shock was delivered.
